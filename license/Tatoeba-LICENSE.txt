Attribution (as required by CC-BY 2.0 FR)
------------------------------------------------------------
The graded sentence bank in assets/synstudy.db (`sentence_bank` table) is a
derived work of Tatoeba's crowdsourced sentence corpus. Individual Tatoeba
sentences remain the property of their respective contributors, who release
them under CC-BY 2.0 FR.

When redistributing assets/synstudy.db you must:

  1. Attribute Tatoeba as the source of the sentences. The synstudy UI already
     does this in the word-detail panel ("句子来自 Tatoeba（CC-BY 2.0 FR）")
     and in the quiz card (source chip).

  2. Include a copy of the CC-BY 2.0 FR license, or a link to it. This file
     fulfills that requirement.

  3. Indicate if changes were made. synstudy filters Tatoeba sentences by
     length, dictionary coverage, and exam-syllabus vocabulary; it also
     attaches Chinese translations via Tatoeba's own cross-language links.

What synstudy does NOT redistribute
------------------------------------------------------------
- No copyrighted 真题 (real exam questions) from CET-4/6, TEM-4/8, TOEFL, or
  GRE are included in the sentence bank or anywhere else in synstudy. Those
  exams are proprietary to their respective testing bodies (教育部考试中心,
  高等学校外语专业教学指导委员会, ETS) and cannot be legally redistributed.
- The `sentence_bank.exam_levels` column contains only our own derived tags
  computed from open ECDICT syllabus metadata; no exam content is copied.
