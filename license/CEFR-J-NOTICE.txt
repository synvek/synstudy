CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile — Terms of use (attribution required)
==============================================================

Source dataset
--------------
Open Language Profiles — English datasets from CEFR-J
https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj

Used in this project to populate the `words.cefr` column (A1-C2 difficulty
levels). Files consumed:
  * cefrj-vocabulary-profile-1.5.csv        (levels A1-B2)
  * octanove-vocabulary-profile-c1c2-1.0.csv (levels C1/C2)
